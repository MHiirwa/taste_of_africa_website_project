Isombe

Rwanda's beloved stew of pounded cassava leaves, creamy with peanut butter.

⏱ 1 hr 30 min 🍽 Serves 4

About this dish

Isombe is a comforting Rwandan dish of pounded cassava leaves simmered with onions, eggplant and groundnuts (peanuts). It is rich in greens and traditionally served with rice, ugali or plantains. Cassava leaves must always be cooked thoroughly, which is why isombe simmers slowly until tender.

Ingredients

Servings: 4
  • 500 g cassava leaves, pounded (or kale / spinach)
  • 1 eggplant, diced
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 green pepper, chopped
  • 3 tbsp palm or vegetable oil
  • 4 tbsp smooth peanut butter
  • 2 cups water
  • Salt to taste
  • Smoked fish, optional

Cooking instructions

Step-by-step method
  1. Rinse the cassava leaves well. Boil them in water for 30 minutes (fresh leaves must be cooked thoroughly), then drain.
  2. Heat the oil and fry the onion, garlic and green pepper for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the eggplant and cook for 5 more minutes, then add the drained cassava leaves and the water.
  4. Cover and simmer on low heat for 30-40 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Stir in the peanut butter and salt and simmer for 10 more minutes until creamy.
  6. Serve hot with rice, ugali or boiled plantains.
Chef's tips

Taste and adjust the seasoning at the end. Use the servings buttons above to scale the ingredients, and keep the heat low whenever a recipe says to simmer.
