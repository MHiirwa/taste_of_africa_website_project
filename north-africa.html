North Africa

Where the Sahara meets the Mediterranean, North African food blends Berber, Arab, Ottoman and Mediterranean influences into fragrant, gently spiced dishes.

Regions › North Africa

Food culture

Where: Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Libya and Egypt.

Sweet and savoury sit side by side here: apricots with lamb, honey with spice. Meals often begin with small salads and bread and end with mint tea, a symbol of hospitality.

Staple ingredients

  • Couscous, wheat and flatbreads
  • Olives and olive oil
  • Dates, figs and almonds
  • Chickpeas, lentils and fava beans
  • Lamb, chicken and preserved lemons

Cooking techniques

  • Slow-cooking in a clay tagine
  • Steaming couscous over a simmering stew
  • Baking bread in communal wood-fired ovens
  • Grilling meat over charcoal
  • Preserving lemons and olives in salt

Signature spices & flavours

  • Ras el hanout
  • Saffron
  • Cumin
  • Cinnamon
  • Harissa
