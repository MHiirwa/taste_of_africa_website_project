About us

Promoting African food heritage, one recipe at a time.

Our mission

To celebrate, document and share Africa's food heritage, so that the recipes, ingredients and stories of every region are cooked, enjoyed and passed on.

Tastes of Africa is a student project that brings together recipes, videos and regional guides in one place. We want to show that African cuisine is not one thing, but a continent of traditions.

A brief history of African cuisine

  1. Ancient grains

    Africans domesticated sorghum, millet, teff and African rice thousands of years ago, forming the base of many cuisines today.

  2. Trade routes

    Trans-Saharan caravans carried salt, dates and spices north and south, while Indian Ocean trade brought cloves, cardamom and rice to the Swahili coast.

  3. New ingredients

    From the 1500s, crops from the Americas such as maize, cassava, tomatoes, chillies and groundnuts arrived and were woven into local cooking. Imagine jollof without tomatoes!

  4. Diaspora and the world

    African cooks and ingredients shaped the foods of the Caribbean, Brazil and the southern United States. Today, restaurants, chefs and food bloggers share African dishes with the whole world.

A cultural food landmark: Jemaa el-Fnaa, Marrakech

This famous square in Marrakech turns into an open-air kitchen every evening, with stalls serving tagines, grilled meats, soups and sweets.
